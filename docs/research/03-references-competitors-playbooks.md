**The gap Skreed exploits:** every competitor sells prints/designs or protection. None sells shade as the product. Market: mobile cases USD 26.18B in 2026 → 47B by 2036 (futuremarketinsights). The teaser should look like a paint brand or Pantone, not a case brand.

## 3. Pre-launch / waitlist playbooks (numbers)

- **Harry's (2013):** 100,000 emails in 1 week; 77% from referrals; two-page microsite (email → personal share link); tiers 5/10/25/50 referrals. tim.blog; referralrock.com [S]. Skreed analog: 3 = early access, 10 = free matte case, 25 = name a shade / limited gloss, 50 = full family set.
- **Robinhood (2013):** ~1M waitlist; email only; live queue position; refer to move up; no money. viral-loops.com; getwaitlist.com [S].
- **Dropbox:** 100K → 4M in 15 months via two-sided rewards. growsurf.com [S].
- **Mailbox (2013):** 380K at launch → 1.25M in 3 weeks; reservation counter showing people ahead/behind. venturebeat; phonearena [S].
- **Superhuman:** 180K waitlist; invite as status symbol. insidehook [S].
- **Monzo:** gamified queue + "Golden Ticket" skip-the-queue drove ~40% of 2017 signups at zero cost. tomblomfield.com; waitlister.me [S].
- **Glossier (2014):** pre-launch IG mood board hit 13K followers in ~1 week with no product; 70% of sales from peer recs. extole.com [S].
- **Rhode (2022):** 3 SKUs under $30; sold out in 3 days; 440K waitlist for one product; $10M in 11 days. optimonk; femfounded [S].
- **Nothing Phone (1) India 2022:** community invite codes 48 h; 100K+ waitlist in 2 days; serialized first-100 auctioned; ₹2,000 refundable pass; 10M "notify me"; 100K units in 20 days. screenrant; techradar [S]. Phone (3a) Community Edition co-designed with 4 community winners; Delhi "Drops". nothing.community [S].
- **boAt:** bootstrapped ₹30L; lifestyle positioning; Shark Tank +60% recall at zero spend. eflot [S].
- **Diwali 2026 (verified):** Dhanteras Fri Nov 6, Diwali Sun Nov 8, Bhai Dooj Tue Nov 10. calendardate.com; awarenessdays.com [S]. The Nov 4 flip lands 2 days before Dhanteras: the "gift your shade" window.
- **India WhatsApp festive data:** 83% of WhatsApp-driven orders in Oct–Dec 2025 were first-time buyers; automated journeys CTR 11.1% vs broadcasts 2.6%. 4-phase plan: teaser → early bird → peak rush → last-minute. campaignhq.co; wati.io [S].

**Legal notes [S]:** free-entry, no-purchase giveaways are not lotteries; purchase-tied draws fall under Prize Competitions Act 1955 + Consumer Protection Act 2019; Tamil Nadu bans lucky-draw contests (standard T&Cs exclude TN); prefer a skill element ("name this shade"). DPDP Act 2023 / Rules notified 13 Nov 2025: per-purpose affirmative consent (waitlist ≠ promo WhatsApp); withdrawal as easy as opt-in; records; core obligations by 13 May 2027. Separate checkboxes for email vs WhatsApp updates.

**Mechanics menu:** referral tiers (Harry's), position counter (Robinhood/Mailbox), shade reservation ("x people reserved Teal 07"), golden-ticket skip-the-queue (Monzo), serialized first-100 (Nothing), countdown to Nov 3 + Dhanteras, WhatsApp 4-phase, IG broadcast channel.

## 4. Instagram growth mechanics tied to a website

- No off-site follow API; buttons only deep-link. Universal link `https://instagram.com/<handle>` opens the app when installed. `instagram://user?username=` iOS-only; Android needs `intent://`. joturl; sharethis [S].
- DM links: `ig.me/m/<handle>` opens a DM thread; use for "DM us your shade". creatorflow.so [S].
- Broadcast channels: invite link shareable in Stories sticker, bio, DMs, any website; needs creator/business account. about.instagram.com; setsmart.io [S].
- Close Friends drops: VIP launches/early access, 2–4 VIP stories/week. later.com [S].
- "Follow to unlock": Meta's Spam standard prohibits requiring engagement to access exclusive content; Graph API can't verify follows. Follow-to-enter giveaways appear allowed per Meta's promotion table but newer guidance says engagement can be encouraged, not mandatory; always add the Instagram release and official rules. tryunlockdm; contedy; gleam [S]. Recommendation: "Follow for a bonus entry" + a no-follow entry path.
- Embedding feed: Basic Display API shut down Dec 2024; Business/Creator via Graph API only. Behold (JSON API + embed), Elfsight (hashtag sources, no token for public profiles); official oEmbed for single posts. behold.so; elfsight.com [S].
- UGC hashtag: one tag (#MyShadeOfSkreed-style), pulled via Elfsight.
- Treat skreed.in mobile view as the link-in-bio destination.

## 5. 2026 design trends relevant to lifestyle brands

- **Kinetic typography:** viewport-scaled type as architecture; GSAP + SplitType; reserve width for widest state. Wodwo, Buddha Brands, Mat Voyce. line25; studiomeyer; creativebloq [S].
- **Bento grids:** modular tiles; ideal for "10 families" on phone. figma.com resource library [S].
- **Liquid Glass:** Apple iOS 26 (WWDC Jun 2025), toned down in iOS 27; web recreations via `backdrop-filter` + SVG `feDisplacementMap`/`feSpecularLighting`. macrumors; freefrontend [S]. Natural fit for Skreed's gloss finish.
- **Kinetic/mesh gradients** replacing static gradients (Vercel fused mesh). fireart.studio [S].
- **Tactile brutalism / grain / retro:** Gumroad canonical; noise as a thin top layer never touching text contrast. setproduct; hubspot [S]. Fit for matte.
- **Colour drenching / dopamine design:** saturated palettes on lifestyle/beauty/youth sites; "Pop Futurism". bubble.io; envato [S]. Pantone Cloud Dancer white as a neutral stage so all colours shine: use as Skreed's canvas.
- **Digital-physical product photography:** Teenage Engineering, Oura Ceramic, Apple scroll-recolour.

## Synthesis
Position as a paint/Pantone brand that happens to make cases: Cloud-Dancer-white canvas, a 10×24 swatch wall as the hero that recolours a single hero case in place (Apple pattern), matte rendered with grain and gloss with liquid-glass refraction, kinetic "240" counter, countdown to Nov 4 positioned 2 days before Dhanteras. Capture with "Reserve your shade" (phone + optional email, separate DPDP consents), a Robinhood-style position + Harry's-style referral tiers, and route IG growth through a universal-link follow button, an ig.me DM link, a broadcast-channel invite, and a skill-based "name this shade" contest (TN excluded, follow = bonus entry only).
