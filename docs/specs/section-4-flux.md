## 1. Goal

After the door and the bubble, the visitor stands in five dreamlike rooms, one at a time, each a looping video that fills the screen (the fog, the water and the gradients Sam named are in the videos themselves) with one of our products turning in front of it, and moves between them with the reference's own step: the next room swings in on a wide arc while the view pinches and relaxes and the lights go down on one room and come up on the next, 0.77 s a step, one step a gesture. The fifth room gives way to the reference's cloud, which carries the page back to the hero; the page is a loop both ways, and the hero scrolled up lands on the fifth room under the same cloud. Every page section settles to a whole screen when the scroll stops across a seam. Nothing in the scenes looks like a bathroom, nothing is a panel or a button, and the phone gets its own portrait set of the same five rooms on its own, narrower arc.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

1. **The sequence (B1, B2, B4).** On the 3D path at 390 x 844 and 1280 x 800: the walk, the press, the bubble with scene 1 inside it, scene 1 at rest with its product face out and its four lines, five scenes by five steps (each 0.77 s plus or minus one frame, the arc of its class, the fov 45 to 42 to 45 with the return at 0.65, the front switch at 0.75, the lights down and up, the edge-on swap), the hold, the exit seam to the hero; the way back at scene 1 into the hall; the wrap onto scene 5 under the cloud. Driven through `__s4` and the stills script; frames at 390 and 1280 for Sam, including a mid-step frame of each class.
2. **Approved content only (F2, rule 38).** Production carries the ten approved loops by hash, the four products in the shades, sizes and poses of section 3, no placeholder; scene 5 shows the phone case until the lanyard model is approved.
3. **The step is whole (D2, E3).** In `world` and in range every wheel tick, key and one-finger swipe (decided on its first move and cancelled from it) is one step; a gesture during a step or within 150 ms after is swallowed; the wheel burst filter holds (200 ms, 0.5); Ctrl+wheel, multi-touch and any gesture at visualViewport.scale above 1.01 are never captured; at scene 5 a forward gesture scrolls the page; at scene 1 a backward gesture runs the way back; the page's q is snapped to the scene's q when a step ends; a position change without a gesture re-syncs the scene; `hall` never captures.
4. **The glue (D2, E3).** In each of the three spans, a visitor's run that stops in the lower half settles to the span's start and in the upper half to its end, in 0.5 s, on wheel, keys and touch, after 150 ms still with no finger down, once per run, cancelled by new input; instant under reduced motion; never after focus, scrollIntoView or find, never with a typing control focused, the keyboard up or the page zoomed; never inside the walk, the cascade, the pull-back or the carousel. Tested with innerHeight not equal to 100svh.
5. **No flash (E3, WCAG 2.3.1).** Over every loop as drawn, every step, five steps back to back at Tune's shortest stepMs, both seams, the way back and the wrap, at 390 x 844 and 1280 x 800: no 341 x 256 CSS px region has more than 25 percent of its area flash more than three times in any one second, a flash being a pair of opposing changes of at least 10 percent of the maximum relative luminance where the darker state is below 0.80, the same for saturated red. Recorded per loop in `room-flux.json` and the evidence doc.
6. **The loop both ways (B4).** The exit seam runs when the raw scroll crosses q 12.8 downward in `world` or `hall`, holds the scroll, jumps to scroll 0 under full cover and resets to the hall; at scroll 0 with the intro over, a touch from scroll 0 moving 16 px down or a new wheel burst runs the reverse seam onto scene 5 with the hero's followers snapped; a gesture during the intro lock, a burst already under way, pull-to-refresh and any key do nothing; focus and the live region follow (wrapLive).
7. **Video policy and the build (C1, E3).** media-src 'self' in every build's CSP; loops stream by URL, muted, no audio track, playsinline; no video under reduced motion, Save-Data, 2g or slow-2g, deviceMemory 2 or less (stills on the planes, no loading line); a refused or late loop keeps the still and is retried on the next input; a loop the browser paused resumes on return (the resume rule of section 4); the five elements are unlocked inside the press task and after a wrap; one class per visit; one WebGL context on the page.

9. **Nothing of the reference ships (A4).** The guard bans the names and strings of section 5 in `src/` and `public/`; the capture is untracked and excluded; the evidence doc states the method and provenance.
10. **Plain text (A3, rule 25).** The unit test on room.css and Room.astro: no background but none, --night or --skeleton, no border outside :focus-visible, no box-shadow or backdrop-filter, border-radius 0, no rm-pill, btn or txtbtn; the lines in Plex Mono capitals through the decode, sentence case in the hidden copy; the bottom shade lives in the carousel's shader, not the DOM.

## 9. Decisions taken

- **X-1 The arc per class.** The reference's 80 degree arc is built for a wide frame. With a vertical fov of 45 a portrait stage sees only about 11 degrees to each side, and ray-casting the reference's rig over the step shows the outgoing plane fully off screen by 20 degrees and the incoming one back only at 20 degrees: an empty frame for about 0.3 s of every step at 390 x 844. The portrait arc is 22 degrees (the planes' edges hand over in the middle of the step), a Tune key, inferred because the reference's phone chunk was not captured; the wide arc is 80 as the reference, whose rig bottoms at 69.0 percent coverage at 1280 x 800 near eased 0.68 (the outgoing plane 12 percent, the incoming 57). A unit test pins plane coverage at or above 90 percent on the portrait arc and 65 on the wide. The clear colour behind the arc is black, the reference's.
- **X-2 The camera is fixed and the planes orbit.** The reference's camera never rotates; the two planes swing on the ring and billboard toward it while the fov pinches. Sam's "rotation of perspective" is the fov pinch plus the arc.
- **X-3 The cloud is the exit and the wrap; the bubble is the entry.** In the reference the fbm cloud covers section seams, not steps. Our entry seam is the bubble Sam kept; the exit and the reverse (the wrap) are the reference's seam with its values (280 in, 360 out, 400 lock, 16 px). The cloud's light is Pearl Whisper rather than #ffffff (rule 23); its shadow tone is the reference's.
- **X-4 The lights per step are the reference's intro-less path.** Our loops have no intro or outro clips, so the plane material carries the reference's light-up shader (proximity through startAt 0.5, softness 0.64, boost 0.17, floor 0.01, rate dt over 0.68 s): rooms dim and relight on every step as they do there. No clips are added.
- **X-5 Native scroll stays the page's position; the capture is local.** Sam accepted a wrapped virtual scroll; what the brief needs is the loop both ways, whole steps in the carousel and the glue. Those are built on native scroll: non-passive wheel and touch listeners attached only while the raw position is in range in `world` or at scroll 0, decided on the first move of a touch, settles by scrollTo. The quiz's native scrolling, find-in-page, focus scrolling, bfcache and the hero's followers stay as they are, and there is no third smoother. Recorded as E-S4-scroll.

The spans are the visible seams, not the DOM tops; a scroll that stops in a scrubbed range (the pull-back, the cascade, the walk) is where the visitor left it.
