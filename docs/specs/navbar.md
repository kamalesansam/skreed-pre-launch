## 1. Goal

One line at the top of the landing page, fixed in place on every screen, that names the page's four sections and jumps to any of them in one press. It serves the brand goal "make the 240 shades easy to reach": the families and the quiz are 14 and 17 screens down a scroll that cannot be skimmed today. CHECKLIST G22 already expects "the sticky section index" doing the job breadcrumbs would; this is that index, with today's four sections in place of the old plan's eight.

## 2. Wireframe in words

**390 px, at rest (after the intro).** The logotype at the top left, unchanged. At the top right, on its row, one word: "Menu" (Work Sans Light in capitals since 2026-11-03, E-NAV-font, Open Sans 600 before; the label size, Pearl Whisper, a 44 px hit row, no box, its text ending on `--inset` as the logotype starts on it). There is no bar fill, no hairline, no blur and no shadow, so the scene runs edge to edge as approved.

**390 px, Menu pressed.** A light drawer slides in from the right edge (the popover attribute; Escape native): a panel 75 percent of the screen wide and the screen's full height, over 300 ms (`--dur-card`) on `--ease-standard`, and back out the same way; at once under reduced motion. The popover itself is a transparent full-screen layer with the panel at its right, so the strip of page left in view beside the panel belongs to the layer: a tap there only closes the drawer and never presses what is under it (an answer, Begin, the logotype), and wheel and touch on the drawer stay out of the page's own gestures. The drawer closes if the window widens past 768 px, where Menu goes. The family switcher's layout: a 56 px head row with "Sections" at the left and "Close" at the right, where Menu was, then one 56 px row per section between hairlines: "Home", "Families", "Quiz", "Products", and a last row, "About", a plain link to the About page (`/about/`, D7). Pearl Whisper ground, Urban Slate words and hairlines (the page's light pair; "bright grey or something, not black"), a hairline on its left edge where it meets the scene, which stays in view beside it. The section on screen is underlined and set at 600, and carries `aria-current="location"`. A press on a section row closes the drawer and jumps; a press on About leaves for its page, the drawer closed as the page goes (D7).

**1280 px (from 768 px).** No Menu: the five words in a row at the top right on the logotype's row (the four sections, then About, a plain link to `/about/`; D7), each a 44 px tall hit with 12 px of padding at its sides and 24 px (`--s4`) between the hits, the last word's text ending on `--inset`. The section on screen is underlined (2 px, 3 px offset).

**The jump.** The press fades the `--night` veil in over 250 ms, the page jumps in place to the section's rest position, the scene's followers snapped, and the veil lifts once the scene has drawn two frames: the jump Back to top made, pointed at four places instead of one. No smooth scroll through the sections between. A press while a jump is under way is never lost: the latest one runs under the same veil if it came before the lift, or as a jump of its own once the lift is over. Reduced motion: the cut with no veil.

**Home and Back to top.** The index sits where Back to top sat on the landing page, and Home makes exactly its jump (section 1 in place, no reload, keyboard focus on the logotype), always in view on the wide layout and one row of the drawer on phones. So the landing page draws no Back to top control; `BackToTop.astro` stays mounted there for the veil and for the way back from a family page (its press restores the landing page from the back-forward cache and jumps to section 1). The family pages keep Back to top as it is.

**Products** is always listed ("its okay add products as well no problem"). While the quiz gate holds section 4 (E-QG-lock), a press on Products opens it exactly as Skip quiz does (`gate.ts` `unlock`): `hidden` and `data-locked` go, `data-open="nav"`, the visit's flag is written (`skreed:quiz-done`, so it stays open for the visit), `room_unlock { by: nav }` is tracked and the hero hears `skreed:quiz-unlock` and loads the scenes (the hall until 2026-11-02); on the 3D path the page waits for the scenes under the veil, at most 1.5 s (Skip quiz's own wait), and lands on scene 1 fully in: the jump snaps section 4's own followers to q 1 as well (`RoomDirector.reset(y)`, which does nothing while the section has no reading, as it has none behind the gate), so the third wipe does not play again after the veil lifts; and a press on an open section whose scenes have not arrived waits for them the same way, so scene 1's reveal does not play after the lift either. Once open, Products only jumps.

**Tone.** The bar's words take the logotype's colour, as Back to top did: Pearl Whisper, and Urban Slate while the logotype turns Urban Slate over the light fog or a seam's cloud. The drawer is always Pearl Whisper with Urban Slate.

"Families" is the family pages' own word for the switcher. "Home" and "Products" are the brief's words (2026-11-02). "About" is the About page's own title ("About Skreed", `docs/specs/about.md` section 3), there by that page's brief, "just add it in the navbar" (2026-11-02; D7). No icon, no number, no badge: Menu is a word, as every control on the site is ("All families", "Skip quiz"), so no icon set comes in (rules 10 and 22).

## 4. Motion plan

No library: the Web Animations API for the veil, as Back to top had it, and a CSS transition for the drawer (a `transform` from `translateX(100%)`, entering from `@starting-style`, `display` and the top layer held for the way out). GSAP is allowed, but neither needs it. Hover lifts a word 1 px and a press scales it to 0.98 over `--dur-press` on `--ease-standard` (the `.press` pattern of "All families" and Back to top). Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the drawer opens and closes at once, the lift and press scale are off and the jump has no veil.

## 5. Data and states

- Input: the scroll position, the tier (`html.hero3d` without `html.poster` is the 3D path), section 4's `data-locked` and `data-tier`.
- Rest positions. 3D path: Home 0 (the hero's `__skreedTop`); Families section 2 at rest, `S2T.LAND` screens (the return path's position); Quiz the top of `section#find` (the end of the second seam's glue); Products section 4's q 1, its top plus one stage height (Skip quiz's landing, `rideTarget`), or its top when section 4 shows its stills. Poster tier: the top, `nav#families` centred (as `boot.ts` centres it), the quiz's top and section 4's top.
- Current section. 3D path: Home until the middle of the first wipe, Families until the middle of the second seam, Quiz until section 4's top, then Products. Poster tier: the section under the middle of the screen. While section 4 is locked, the page ends at the quiz, so Products is never current.
- Focus. A keyboard press moves focus into the section two frames after the jump (the jump resets section 4 for its next lap, and `walk.ts`'s new lap blurs any focus inside `section#room` on the island's next frame): the logotype, the first family link, the quiz's live question (the card `deck.ts` focuses to announce it) or else its first control on screen (Begin, the result's first link), section 4's pad on the 3D path (whose focus the arrival at scene 1 moves on to scene 1's name, `walk.ts`) or scene 1's name on the poster tier; each candidate is tried until one takes focus, so a hidden or inert part (the quiz keeps a hidden card's box) is never chosen. A pointer press leaves focus nowhere in particular (the pressed word is blurred).
- States. Loading: hidden with the logotype under the intro (`html.uh1`) and under the loader's screen. Busy: a press during a jump runs after it (above). During Skip quiz's ride (`#room[data-ride]`, its wait for the scenes included, at most 2.7 s) a press waits and runs once the ride has landed or been ended (a touch on the index ends it), so it is neither lost nor undone by the ride's next frame. Under reduced motion the jumps still run one at a time, in order, with no veil; a press on Products that waits for the scenes marks its word `aria-busy` meanwhile. No JavaScript: the links are plain fragment links (`#top`, `#families`, `#find`, `#room`) over the poster layout, which is in flow, and nothing is locked; from 768 px they are inline as with a script, and under 768 px Menu is not shown and the five words (About last) sit in a second row under the logotype's row, the last ending on `--inset` (a drawer opened without a script would stay open over the section it jumped to). No popover in the browser (inside the build's own target: Safari 16.4 to 16.6, Chrome 111 to 113, Firefox 114 to 124): the same second row, and the script never touches the popover API (`:popover-open` is not a selector there, and `matches()` would throw on it), so every section word jumps and About leaves for its page. Error: a jump with no island (a failed or soft poster) is a plain instant scroll; a press on Products whose scenes do not arrive in 1.5 s lands anyway, as Skip quiz's does. Empty, offline, validation, permission: n/a (no data, no input).
- About. The last link in the bar and in the drawer is a page link, `/about/`, with `data-page` and no `data-sec`: the script never jumps or marks it current, a press is the browser's own navigation (no veil, no smooth scroll); the drawer is closed as the page leaves (`pagehide`), so the landing page enters the back-forward cache without it and Back brings it back shut, and a restore that still finds it open closes it (`pageshow`); `nav { to: about }` is tracked as the sections' presses are, a plain press only (a modifier press opens a new tab, untracked, as theirs is). Without JavaScript, and without the popover API, it is a plain link in the same row. Since 2026-11-03 the About page carries the index too, with no script (D7, AC12); its Products is a plain link to `/#room`, and an arrival with that fragment makes the press Products makes here once the loader has lifted (D7).

## 6. Budget

At most 3 KB gz of JS in a deferred module (no change to the critical boot chunk), at most 2 KB gz of CSS, no images, no fonts. With About and the drawer's close on the way to its page (D7, 2026-11-02): the index's chunk 2.2 KB gz (5,197 bytes raw), critical JS 18.6 KB, total JS 210.5 KB, 243.3 KB with section 4.
