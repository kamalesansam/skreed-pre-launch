## 1. Goal

After the ten gems, the visitor answers six quick questions by swiping and gets one of the 240 shades as theirs, on the spot, with the way to see it on the case; then leaves an email for the gift. Brand goals: 1 (a brand people connect with) and 3 (lead capture); the concept catalog's highest-converting mechanic (#8).

**Tap.** A click or tap on an option commits that side (`commit(side, 0)`), unless the click is the one the browser fires after a drag (swallowed, below).

**Drag (Pointer Events on `#qzCards`; touch, mouse and pen alike).** `pointerdown` with button 0 on the top card, while no commit is running, records the pointer, its start point and its time. On `pointermove`: under 8 px of horizontal travel (`DRAG_DEAD`) nothing happens; if at that moment the vertical travel exceeds the horizontal, the gesture is abandoned (no capture; the page keeps panning through `touch-action: pan-y`); otherwise the drag starts and the card takes `setPointerCapture`. A velocity estimate runs on each `pointermove` (`vel = 0.7 * vel + 0.3 * (the movement since the last event / its dt)`, px per ms; `deck.ts` line 125). Once per animation frame `applyDrag` sets the card's transform to `translateX(dx) rotate(8 * p deg)` with `p = clamp(dx / (0.35 * width), -1, 1)` (up to 8 degrees at 35 percent of the card's width, leaning into the drag, pivoting at the bottom centre); at `p <= -1` the left option and at `p >= 1` the right option get `.is-lift` (translateY -1 px and an underline at a 3 px offset). On `pointerup`: a release at 35 percent of the card's width or more (`COMMIT_FRACTION`), or with `|vel| >= 0.5` px/ms (`FLICK`), commits the side the card moved to (left is bit 1, right is bit 0); anything shorter springs back (transform to `none`, 300 ms `DUR_CARD`, `EASE_REBOUND`, `fill: 'forwards'` then cancelled). `pointercancel` and `lostpointercapture` spring back. Once a drag has started (the pointer crossed the 8 px dead zone), a release 8 px or more from the start, or 300 ms or more after the press (`TAP_MS`), sets `swallow` for the rest of the task, so the click the browser fires on the option under the pointer is ignored: one answer per drag (`deck.ts` lines 132 and 133; `endDrag` returns before that line when no drag started). A press that never crosses the dead zone, however long it is held, is a tap and commits through its click.

**Commit.** `commit()` stores the answer, tracks `quiz_answer { q, side }`, and flies the card off: from its current transform to `translateX(dir * (1.3 * width + |dx|)) rotate(dir * 14deg)` (dir is -1 for left, +1 for right), 300 ms `DUR_CARD`, `EASE_IN`, `fill: 'forwards'`, opaque all the way (it covers the next card until it has left), then cancelled, the card `hidden`, `top` advanced. The next card comes forward from `scale(0.96)` to `none` in 300 ms `EASE_STANDARD` (no opacity ramp), is announced ("Question 2 of 6: Warm or cool?") and focused. `overflow-x: clip` on the section keeps the translated, rotated card out of the scrollable overflow, so the page never gains a horizontal scroll range during a drag or a fly-off.

**Keyboard.** With the top card focused (`role="group"`, `tabindex="0"`; the option buttons inside it are in the tab order and their keydown bubbles to the card), ArrowLeft commits the left side and ArrowRight the right side (`preventDefault`). Tab reaches the two option buttons, which commit on Enter or Space as any button does. The tab order through the page on the 3D path: the corner logotype, the ten family links (`nav#families`, fixed over the viewport and in the tab order by the family-page contract; focusing one snaps the scroll back to the gems; present only in builds with `FAMILY_PAGES` on, section 10, 5), then section 3's controls.

## 4. Motion plan

**Libraries.** None new, no GSAP. The hand-off is the hero island's second wipe (the v11 composite) with its far side changed to the fog ground. The DOM motion is the Web Animations API on transform and opacity, CSS transitions on transform and `text-decoration-color`, driven by Pointer Events (one handler for touch, mouse and pen). Reduced motion comes from `src/scripts/motion.ts` (`reducedMQ`, read live before every animation) and from the `prefers-reduced-motion` block in `quiz.css`. The constants in `deck.ts` twin DESIGN.md's tokens: `DUR_OUT` 200 ms (`--dur-out`), `DUR_CARD` 300 ms (`--dur-card`), `DUR_REDUCED` 150 ms, `EASE_STANDARD` `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` (`--ease-standard`), `EASE_IN` `cubic-bezier(.55, .085, .68, .53)` (`--ease-in`), `EASE_REBOUND` `cubic-bezier(0, .3, .5, 1)` (`--ease-rebound`); the rise and the result's entrance are 400 ms literals; the hover and press transitions use `--dur-press` (150 ms).

**Reduced motion, exactly as `deck.ts` and `quiz.css` do it.** `reduced()` is `reducedMQ.matches`, read at each call. The intro fades out in 150 ms (`DUR_REDUCED`, `EASE_STANDARD`) and the deck does not rise. A commit fades the card out (opacity 1 to 0, 150 ms, `fill: 'forwards'`, the default easing) instead of flying it off; the next card fades in (opacity 0 to 1, 150 ms, `EASE_STANDARD`) instead of scaling. During a drag the card does not follow (`card.style.transform` is left alone); `.is-lift` still toggles past the threshold, and the CSS removes its 1 px transform, so the underline alone marks the side that will commit; release past the threshold still commits. The spring-back runs at 0 ms. The result panel appears with no animation; `retry()` scrolls with `behavior: 'instant'`. The CSS sets `transition: none` on the primary buttons, the text buttons and the options and `transform: none` on `.is-lift`, the option's hover and press, and the primary button's hover and press, so no control moves at all. On the poster tier (reduced motion's default) there is no island, so no fog; on `still3d` the fog is frozen at `uTime` 7 and the composite's `uCalm` 1 removes the wipe's parallax and aberration.

**WCAG 2.2.2.** Nothing in the section's DOM moves on its own. The fog under the section is the island's and drifts for as long as the section is in view, which is longer than 5 s with no pause control; that is part of the pending exception E-S3-bg (section 10, 3), as the hero's own drift is part of the pending E-2.2.2.
