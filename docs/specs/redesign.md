## 1. Goal

One sentence: the landing page becomes the long editorial page of the PDF's page 1 (photographs, big headlines, the 240 shades as the argument), alive with GSAP scroll motion, carrying the parts the brief keeps (logotype with its glitch, the section index, the countdown, the quiz with its result, About on its own page), with the jars closing the page as they closed the old one and on a page of their own as well.

It serves the brand goals "colour is personal" (the shades are the story of every section) and the sign-up (one place to leave an email, the jars, at the landing page's end and on `/jars/`; the PDF's two steps left the page with D19).

## 2. Decisions

**D1 Routes.** `/` is the new landing; the jars are its last section too (D18). `/jars/` is new: the current section 5, the jars, as built, on a page of its own (the gift in the section index opens it). `/about/`, `/shades/<family>/`, `/privacy/`, `/terms/`, `/unsubscribe/` and the 404 are unchanged. The sitemap gains `/jars/`.

**D2 The old landing's code stays on disk, unmounted.** The hero island, section 2's gems, section 4's scenes and their assets are not imported by any page of this branch, so the build emits none of them. Their source files are not edited or deleted here: the family pages share modules with them (`hero/section4/scroll.ts`, `config/hero.ts`, the loader), the quiz's tests read `section2/labels.ts`, and one `git revert` or a branch switch must be able to bring the old landing back. The running old site is the archive ref.

**D3 The shell on `/`.** No Gate, no Loader, no SiteNote, no HeroStage, no `boot.ts`, no `shell.css`. The page mounts `WordmarkSprite`, `SiteLogo` (unchanged: its glitch installs itself and bursts 0.75 s after load), `SiteNav` (markup and CSS unchanged; its targets and its map are the new page's, D4) and `BackToTop page="home"` for the jump's veil. A one-line inline script first in `<head>` puts `js` on `<html>` (the quiz, the nav's drawer and the countdown need it). The `.site-logo` rules and `.sr-only` are copied into `redesign.css`, as `about.css` carries its own copy.

**D4 The section index.** Items and order as today (Home, Families, Quiz, Products, About, the gift; "keep the logo and navbar intact"). Targets: Home `#top`, Families `#families`, Quiz `#quiz`, Products `#products`, About `/about/`, the gift `/jars/` (a page link, as About is). A press on a section item jumps in place under the veil, as today. The item for the section on screen is underlined: Home over the hero and "what you've seen so far"; Families over `#families` and the picker; Products over `#products`, MATCH ESSENTIALS and the five tiles; Quiz over `#quiz` and the open quiz; none below it. The nav's map is rewritten for ordinary sections (document tops, no scroll map, no `config/hero.ts` import).

**D5 Tone.** The logotype and the nav are Pearl Whisper over photographs and the night quiz, Urban Slate over the light sections. Every section carries `data-tone="dark"` or `"light"`; `scripts/redesign/tone.ts` reads the section under the logotype's row on scroll and resize and calls `window.__skreedLogo.setTone` (the nav copies it, as it does today). Over the quiz's result `html[data-qz-ground]` keeps them Pearl Whisper, as built.

**D6 Grounds and inks, as the PDF.** White `#FFFFFF`, the PDF's warm white `#FEFEFA`, Pearl Whisper `#F7F6F3` under the picker, photographs under a flat black veil at the PDF's strength, `--night` under the quiz and the footer. Ink: black `#000000` headlines, `#3A3A3A` body, `#EFEBE5` on photographs. The PDF's grey line is `#999999` (2.85:1 on white); it ships as `#8C8C8C` (3.36:1, AA for its size), the one tone moved, by under a twentieth. The accent words keep the PDF's own colours: PHONE CASE `#A4BD97`, SOMETHING `#5E97AF`, OVER and the CTA's pill `#D0A5FA`. The three blobs are the PDF's `#363636`, `#394E77` and `#522723` (they stand for the colours "you've seen so far", so they are not catalog shades). The family swatches and the picker's colours are the PDF's own where the PDF shows them (D19, item 4; D21); their ids and names are the catalog's.

**D7 Type** (E-RD-font). Headlines: Inter 500 in capitals (the PDF's Neue Montreal Medium), `font-optical-sizing: auto`, set in the PDF's line breaks at every width, tracked so each line's width matches the PDF's share of the page (section 4's table gives each line's width). Body: Questrial 400. The two Helvetica lines and the labels in Open Sauce One: Inter 700 and 400. DIFFERENT: Rubik Black at rest (D20), and Rubik Glitch, the PDF's own face, only while a glitch burst runs. MATCH ESSENTIALS: Skreed Wide (Krona One, cut and renamed). The blobs' words: WindSong. The picker's line: Poppins 400 italic (in the repo for the quiz result). The countdown keeps its dot digits and Plex Mono labels; the nav keeps Work Sans Light. Inter, Questrial and Poppins italic are already in `fonts.css`; the four new faces are declared in `redesign.css` so only this page ships them.

**D8 The headline scale.** The PDF is 1024.5 pt wide. One unit `u` is a 1024.5th of the page's width, capped where the page is 1600 px wide: `--u: min(100vw, 1600px) / 1024.5`. Every size in section 4 is in `u`. Full-bleed bands stay full width beyond 1600 px; text columns centre in the 1600 px frame. Under 720 px wide the phone layout applies (section 5).

**D9 Pictures.** Cut from the PDF by `scripts/redesign/extract-pdf.py` and encoded by `scripts/redesign/encode-pictures.mjs` to AVIF with a WebP twin at two or three widths, into `src/assets/redesign/`, tracked in git since 2026-11-10 on Sam's word (asked whether they may go into the public repository: "Commit them"), so a fresh clone builds the page; the masters (`assets-src/redesign/`) stay git-ignored and the PDF itself never goes in. `RdPicture.astro` writes the `<picture>` markup with `width`, `height`, `sizes`, `loading="lazy"` below the first screen and `decoding="async"`. The hero's photograph is preloaded and `fetchpriority="high"`.

**D10 The three clips, as live animation** ("Live animations").
- *The cases* (`#seen`): the twelve case cut-outs are real elements. The scene is held for about 1.2 screens (1.7 on a phone) by a CSS sticky stage, not a ScrollTrigger pin, so the page has its full length from first paint and a page opened or restored below it lands in its place: "what you've seen so far" is on screen at rest, each fan opens from its front case, the three back cases sliding out solid to one, two and three equal steps (D19, item 3: every case opaque throughout, the same step in all three fans, the four spaces at the row's ends and between the fans equal), then "is about to change" comes up word by word. On a phone the three fans are larger than the screen's width and the scrub pans across them, one fan at a time.
- *The swatches* (`#families`): ten brush swatches, the PDF's own six cut from its frame and four more in the same brush (D19, item 4), one per family, pop in left to right, each a link to its family page opened on the shade it stands for (`/shades/<family>/?shade=<nnn>`, leaving `#families` in the history as the gems did, so "Back to main page" lands here). Each carries its family's name and "24 SHADES" (the gems' own words) on hover, focus and, on touch, under the row for the swatch last pressed.
- *The picker* (`#glimpse`): five dots, a radio group (Navy, Powder, Teal, Mint, Sage, in the frame's own colours under the catalog's names, D21), the two products repainted in each from the PDF's one frame. A pointer over a dot changes the shade, with nothing pressed; a tap, a click and the arrow keys do too (D19, item 5). A drawn cursor passes over each dot in turn every 2.6 s while the section is on screen, until the visitor's pointer, finger or keyboard reaches them; the picture changes by a circular reveal from the products' centre, the line and the shade's name change with it.

**D11 The quiz.** `Quiz.astro`, `deck.ts` and `quiz.css` are not edited. The quiz sits in `div#quizHost.rd-quizhost` (ground `--night`, `data-tone="dark"`, `hidden` until asked for) right under the CTA box. "Let's find it" runs under the veil: the host is shown, `#find` is scrolled to the top, `#qzBegin` is pressed (the quiz's own loader queues a press made before its chunk mounts), focus goes where the quiz puts it. The result, its links, the offer band and "Not quite? Try again" are the quiz's own. Without JavaScript the CTA is hidden and the quiz's own noscript line stands in a visible host.

**D12 The landing's sign-up: withdrawn by D19** ("remove that signup section"): the PDF's two steps and the blue photograph with its form are not on the page, their component, stylesheet, script, copy and picture are gone, and the jars (D18) are the page's sign-up. What follows is kept as the record of what was built and removed. (`#steps` since D18, which gave `#signup` back to the jars; its files, classes and config keys keep the name sign-up). The PDF's two steps, then the blue photograph with the line and the form. The form is `mountForm` from `scripts/signup/form.ts` on new markup with the names it needs under the prefix `rs` (D18), behind the same `LEADS` flag as today (on in dev, test and staging builds and in the deploy's build, off in a plain production build, where the line stands alone). The server asks for a first name, the tick and a six-digit code, so the form has them: a first-name pill, the email pill with a round arrow button, the tick with the privacy link, one status line; then the code step and the done line, all the existing strings. The two platform links open in a new tab; the ones opened in this visit are sent as `follows`.

**D13 The countdown** (`#launch`, the PDF's blank white slide). The same markup ids, the same inline script (forked without the cue and loader lines), the same dot digits and glitch, drawn in black on white, centred, large. At zero the live line and its link, as today.

**D14 A footer.** The site had none (no `<footer>` on any branch). A small one closes the page on `--night`, under the jars since D18, and it stays: asked on 2026-11-09 "Keep the small footer under the jars?", having been told that with it the jars' headline passes under the logotype when the page is scrolled to its very end, Sam answered "Keep it". It holds: the tagline, Privacy, Terms, About, the three social links the repo holds (Facebook, YouTube, Instagram; the PDF's header also lists X and Threads, for which the repo has no address), the contact address, the place. Flagged for Sam (section 12).

**D15 The cue.** "Scroll down to see more" with the three chevrons at the foot of the hero, the chevrons on the old cue's 2.5 s cycle (D19, item 2), hidden once the page has scrolled 8 px (E-cue-text carries over).

**D16 Motion** (E-RD-motion). GSAP 3.15.1 and ScrollTrigger, bundled from npm, fetched by `import()` after first paint so they are not critical JS. Every tween is created inside one `gsap.matchMedia()` whose query comes from `scripts/motion.ts` (Reduce motion is not honoured since E-3D-only). No SplitText: headlines are split into lines, words and letters at build by `RdSplit.astro`, so nothing is re-flowed in the browser and the CSP's ban on style attributes holds. The page is complete and readable with no script: an element is given its hidden start state only by GSAP, only once GSAP has loaded, and a 2.5 s failsafe shows everything if it has not. The hero's entrance is CSS alone, so the first paint never waits for the chunk. No scroll is captured or eased: wheel, touch and keys stay the browser's. One scene is held: `#seen`, by a CSS sticky stage (no ScrollTrigger pin anywhere).

**D17 Build and tests.** The post-build guard asks for the ten loops only when the built landing mounts section 4, and for none otherwise. The budget script measures the new landing: critical JS under 60 KB, total JS (every chunk the page can import, GSAP included) under 250 KB, the hero picture under 120 KB, every picture AVIF with its WebP twin. Unit tests that read `index.astro`, the nav map or the build script are amended in the open. The old landing's browser suites have no page to run against on this branch and are parked in `playwright.config.ts` with a note; new suites cover the new page.
