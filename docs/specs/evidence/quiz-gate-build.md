**Item 1, the ride's wait for the hall.** In iteration 1 `window.__skreedHall.load()` was `loadHall()`, which ends in the hall's own `load()`; that returns at once once its load has started (`src/scripts/hero/section4/hall.ts` lines 156 and 157), and it always has by the time the ride asks (the stage observer when the quiz comes into view, then the unlock's listener), so `Promise.race` in gate.ts settled within microtasks, "Loading..." never showed and a cold hall never held the ride.
